# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and interviewers: want to judge Liam's frontend craft and taste within a few minutes.
- Fellow engineers: come for technical articles; reading comfort, search, and wayfinding matter most.
- Friends and Liam himself: moods, 碎念, watch log — a personal record.

## Product Purpose

Liam's personal site: a frontend engineer (MSI) publishing technical articles, long-form posts, moods, short thoughts, portfolio projects, and a watch log. The site itself is also the strongest portfolio piece — its interaction quality is part of the message.

## Positioning

A "detective's notebook" (偵探手記): writing presented as case files and evidence, built by the person whose frontend skill it demonstrates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- SvelteKit (Svelte 5) + Tailwind CSS 4, SQLite via Drizzle, Better Auth, mdsvex Markdown posts plus DB-backed articles/moods/thoughts. Three.js already in use for the background layer.
- Self-hosted via Docker behind Traefik.
- Light and dark themes both supported (user toggle + system fallback); Traditional Chinese content.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the detective's-notebook identity (case-file paper tones, typewriter display face, evidence papers) — confirmed by the user 2026-10-19 — but raise it to its most premium, cinematic rendition.
- User wants strong motion and rich 3D at koimsurai.com's level of intensity, with a lighter fallback on phones.

## Evidence on Hand

Real posts, portfolio projects synced from GitHub, TMDB watch log. No testimonials, metrics, or employer claims beyond "frontend engineer at MSI" may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The site is the portfolio: every interaction should demonstrate craft.
- Reading pages stay readable — atmosphere never sits on top of text.
- Motion must stay smooth on mid-range phones and respect reduced-motion.
